# brag plan: shotr

**What it is:** a phone app that turns screenshots you never act on into the thing you meant to do.
**For:** builders and creators who screenshot hiring posts, playbooks and AI updates, then forget them.
**What sets it apart:** it doesn't just organize. It makes: an X/LinkedIn post, a cold email, a build note or takeaways, in your voice.
**Visual hook:** a wall of dead screenshots, then a shutter flash.
**Real UI shown:** the actual design screens (share sheet, Home, result editor) from the shotr canvas.
**Tone:** polished, restrained (dark, one accent, soft cuts on the beat).
**Share caption:** see share-copy.txt.
**Format:** landscape 1920×1080, 30fps, 21s, music synthesized for this video (A minor, 120 BPM, cuts on beats).
